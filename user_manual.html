01 How to Start Monitoring
1
Enter Patient Name / ID
Type the patient's full name or a unique identifier in the Name / ID field at the top-left of the dashboard. This field is required — it cannot be left blank.
2
Enter Patient Age
Type the patient's age (0–120 years) in the Age field. Numeric values only; decimals are not accepted. Age is used for age-stratified clinical scoring.
3
Select Biological Sex
Use the M / F dropdown to select the patient's biological sex. This is required for certain clinical scoring functions.
4
Click Enter to Begin
Press the blue Enter button (or press Enter on any field). The system will:
  • Generate a unique Patient ID (e.g. PAT-001) and Ambulance ID (e.g. AMB-427)
  • Unlock the waveform and vitals panels
  • Connect to the MQTT broker and begin receiving live sensor data
  • Start the elapsed-time counter and ETA countdown
5
Enter NIBP Manually
Blood pressure (systolic) must be entered manually in the NIBP field in the vitals column. Type the value (e.g. 120) and press Enter or click ✓. This unlocks the Acuity Index, GRU Criticality output, and the View Forecast button.
6
Set Hospital Name (Optional)
Click the hospital name bar at the top of the right column to enter the receiving hospital. Press Enter to save or Escape to cancel.
7
Reset / End Session
Click the circular ↺ refresh button (top-left, next to the patient badge) to end the monitoring session and reset all data. A confirmation prompt will appear.
Buttons available after monitoring begins: e-PCR opens the Electronic Patient Care Report in a new window (auto-refreshes every 5 s). User Manual opens this document. View Forecast opens the XGBoost deterioration forecast (requires NIBP).

04 Clinical Profiles

The rule engine evaluates incoming vitals and matches the patient to one of 12 clinical profiles (highest severity match wins, evaluated top to bottom). Profiles requiring NIBP are skipped until a blood pressure reading is entered. Each card shows: matched conditions, paramedic pre-arrival actions, and hospital clinical orders.

⛔ Critical Cardiogenic Shock HR >100 or <50 · HRV <25 · SBP ≤90 · SpO₂ ≤94
Matched Conditions
  • Tachycardia / Bradycardia
  • Autonomic Failure (HRV↓)
  • Hypotension (SBP ≤90)
  • Hypoxaemia (SpO₂ ≤94)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • IV access ×2; cautious fluid bolus 250 mL if SBP <90 — avoid fluid overload (AHA ACLS 2020)
  • High-flow O₂ 15 L/min via NRB mask; target SpO₂ ≥94% (BTS Oxygen Guidelines)
  • Monitor 12-lead ECG; prepare for immediate defibrillation if VF/VT onset
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • Activate cardiac catheterisation lab (STEMI/cardiogenic shock protocol) — ESC 2023
  • Norepinephrine 0.1–0.2 mcg/kg/min titrated to MAP ≥65 mmHg
  • Urgent ECHO, troponin, BNP, ABG; prepare IABP/Impella if refractory — NICE NG185
⛔ Critical Septic Shock HR >100 · RR >22 · Temp >38.3 or <36.0 · SBP ≤100
Matched Conditions
  • Tachycardia (SIRS)
  • Tachypnoea (SIRS)
  • Temperature Dysregulation
  • Hypotension (qSOFA+)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • IV crystalloid 30 mL/kg over 3 h; reassess lactate after each bolus (Surviving Sepsis 2021)
  • Blood cultures ×2 peripherally before antibiotics if feasible
  • O₂ to target SpO₂ ≥94%; apply CPAP if respiratory failure develops
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • Broad-spectrum IV antibiotics within 1 h — Surviving Sepsis 2021
  • Norepinephrine first-line; target MAP ≥65 mmHg; add vasopressin if refractory
  • Serum lactate, blood cultures ×2, FBC, CRP, PCT, ABG, U&E, LFTs — NICE NG51
⛔ Critical Acute Respiratory Failure SpO₂ ≤91 · RR >25 or RR ≤8
Matched Conditions
  • Severe Hypoxaemia (SpO₂ ≤91)
  • Severe Respiratory Rate Derangement (RR >25 or ≤8)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • High-flow O₂ 15 L/min NRB; prepare BVM; position upright 30–45° (BTS Oxygen)
  • Early BVM ventilation if GCS <8 or SpO₂ unresponsive to O₂ (AHA ACLS 2020)
  • Alert receiving hospital: ETI/RSI team required on arrival — ATLS 10th
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • RSI/intubation if SpO₂ <88% on 15 L O₂ — BTS/ERS Guideline
  • ABG, CXR, FBC, D-dimer/CTPA if PE suspected — NICE NG115
  • Trial NIV (BiPAP) if conscious and cooperative; reassess after 1 h — NICE NG38
⛔ Critical Hypertensive Emergency SBP ≥220 · HR >100 or SpO₂ ≤94
Matched Conditions
  • Hypertensive Emergency (SBP ≥220)
  • Tachycardia
  • Hypoxaemia (SpO₂ ≤94)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • Semi-recumbent position; avoid sudden positional changes; keep calm
  • Do NOT aggressively lower BP — target ≤25% reduction over first hour (ESC 2018)
  • Neurological status Q5 min; note focal deficit, headache, visual change (ATLS)
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • IV labetalol or nicardipine; avoid sublingual nifedipine — ESC 2018
  • Urgent CT head, troponin, echo, U&E, urinalysis
  • Alert stroke/neurology team if focal neurology — NICE NG128
⚠ High Sepsis (No Shock) HR >100 · RR >20 · Temp >38.3 · SBP >100
Matched Conditions
  • Tachycardia (SIRS)
  • Tachypnoea (SIRS)
  • Pyrexia / Fever (SIRS)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • IV access; crystalloid 500 mL bolus if signs of poor perfusion (Surviving Sepsis 2021)
  • Monitor Temp, HR, RR every 5 min; alert ER of suspected sepsis (NEWS2 ≥5)
  • O₂ to maintain SpO₂ ≥94% (BTS Oxygen Guidelines)
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • Blood cultures ×2 then IV antibiotics within 1 h — Surviving Sepsis 2021
  • 30 mL/kg crystalloid if lactate >2 mmol/L or SBP <100 mmHg
  • Sepsis bundle: FBC, CRP, PCT, U&E, LFT, coagulation, CXR — NICE NG51
⚠ High Acute Coronary Syndrome HR >100 · HRV <30 · SpO₂ 92–95
Matched Conditions
  • Tachycardia
  • Reduced HRV (Autonomic Stress)
  • Mild Hypoxaemia (SpO₂ 92–95)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • Aspirin 300 mg PO (if no allergy/contraindication) — AHA ACLS 2020
  • 12-lead ECG; transmit to receiving ER for early STEMI/NSTEMI ID
  • Sublingual GTN 0.4 mg if SBP >100 mmHg; O₂ only if SpO₂ <94% (ESC 2020 ACS)
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • Activate PPCI pathway if STEMI confirmed; door-to-balloon <90 min — ESC 2023
  • Dual antiplatelet: aspirin + ticagrelor 180 mg or prasugrel (per protocol)
  • Troponin T/I, serial ECG, ECHO, CBC, coagulation — ESC ACS Guidelines
⚠ High Severe Asthma / COPD Exacerbation RR >22 · SpO₂ ≤94 · HR >100 · Temp ≤38.3
Matched Conditions
  • Severe Tachypnoea (RR >22)
  • Hypoxaemia (SpO₂ ≤94)
  • Tachycardia (Compensatory)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • Salbutamol 5 mg nebulised ×3; add ipratropium 0.5 mg nebulised (BTS/SIGN 2019)
  • O₂ target SpO₂ 94–98% (88–92% in known COPD) — BTS Oxygen Guidelines
  • Position upright; alert ER: severe exacerbation, consider CPAP/NIV — GINA 2023
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • IV hydrocortisone 100 mg stat or oral prednisolone 40 mg — BTS/SIGN 2019
  • IV magnesium sulphate 2 g over 20 min if life-threatening — GINA 2023
  • Consider ICU/HDU if PEFR <33% or silent chest; ABG, CXR, FBC — NICE NG80
⚠ High Hypothermic Collapse Temp ≤35.0 · HR ≤50 · HRV <30
Matched Conditions
  • Hypothermia (Temp ≤35.0 °C)
  • Bradycardia (HR ≤50)
  • Autonomic Suppression (HRV↓)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • Handle with extreme care — VF risk from physical jostling (ATLS 10th)
  • Passive external rewarming: remove wet clothing, insulate, warm IV fluids 42°C
  • Cardiac monitoring mandatory; defer defibrillation until core Temp >30°C — AHA ACLS 2020
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • Active internal rewarming: warmed IV fluids, warm humidified O₂, bladder irrigation — ATLS
  • ECG for J-waves (Osborn waves); consider ECMO if Temp <28°C
  • Core temperature (oesophageal/rectal), ABG, glucose, electrolytes, coagulation
◈ Moderate Compensated Shock / Pre-Shock HR >100 · SBP 91–110 · SpO₂ ≤95
Matched Conditions
  • Tachycardia
  • Borderline Hypotension (SBP 91–110)
  • Mild Hypoxaemia (SpO₂ ≤95)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • IV access ×2; fluid challenge 500 mL crystalloid; reassess BP and HR (ATLS)
  • Identify and treat reversible cause: haemorrhage, tension pneumothorax, anaphylaxis
  • Elevate legs 15° if no head/spinal injury; O₂ 15 L/min NRB
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • Serial lactate measurements; target <2 mmol/L — Surviving Sepsis 2021
  • Identify shock aetiology: ECHO, CXR, USS, FAST (trauma) — ATLS 10th
  • Vasopressors if fluid-unresponsive; type-and-cross; alert surgical/ICU team
◈ Moderate Autonomic Dysregulation HRV <20 · SBP >90 · SpO₂ >94
Matched Conditions
  • Severe HRV Suppression (Autonomic Failure)
  • Haemodynamically Stable
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • Monitor HR and rhythm continuously; note syncope or position-related symptoms
  • Assess medication history for β-blockers, antiarrhythmics, autonomic agents
  • Maintain supine position if symptomatic; avoid sudden postural changes
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • Cardiology referral for HRV analysis, autonomic function testing, Holter — NICE NG185
  • Tilt-table test if orthostatic hypotension suspected; 12-lead ECG, echo
  • Investigate for underlying causes: diabetes, Parkinson's, amyloidosis — NICE NG28
◈ Moderate Pyrexia / Inflammatory State Temp >38.3 · HR >90 · SBP >100 · SpO₂ >94
Matched Conditions
  • Pyrexia / Fever (Temp >38.3 °C)
  • Tachycardia (Inflammatory)
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • Paracetamol 1 g PO/IV if Temp >38.5 °C and no contraindication (NICE)
  • Increase IV fluid rate; reassess temperature every 15 min
  • Monitor for deterioration to sepsis pattern: rising HR, falling BP, altered consciousness
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • Blood cultures before empirical antibiotics if bacterial source suspected — NICE NG51
  • Sepsis screen: FBC, CRP, PCT, U&E; imaging per suspected source (CXR, USS, CT)
  • Sepsis 6 bundle within 1 h if NEWS2 ≥5 or clinical concern — Surviving Sepsis 2021
✓ Stable Clinically Stable Catch-all — no higher-tier profile matched
Matched Conditions
  • No critical derangement detected
Paramedic Actions (Pre-Arrival)
  • Calculate NEWS2; escalate if ≥7
  • Maintain monitoring; reassess full vital set every 5 min during transport
  • Establish IV access as precaution; document all observations in e-PCR
  • Alert ER of arrival ETA and current NEWS2 score for triage preparation
Hospital Clinical Orders
  • Activate NEWS2 clinical escalation protocol
  • Full clinical assessment in ED; repeat NEWS2 within 30 min of arrival — NICE NEWS2
  • Routine bloods: FBC, U&E, glucose; ECG if age >50 or cardiac history
  • Reassess for clinical deterioration; escalate per local NEWS2 escalation policy
